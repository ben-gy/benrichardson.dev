Common Questions

Is FireWatch AU an official warning service?

No. FireWatch AU is unofficial and not affiliated with any agency. Information may be delayed or incomplete. Always follow the directions of your emergency services, and in an emergency call Triple Zero (000).

How does the app use AI?

The app uses Apple Intelligence, which runs entirely on your device. All AI processing happens locally on your iPhone or iPad, so your data never leaves your device.

Is my data private?

Yes. The app doesn't collect any personal data. Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no data sharing.

Is my data private?

Yes. FireWatch AU has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Your location and saved places stay on your device. The app asks our server for public information such as warnings, fire danger and weather, and sends it nothing about you. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it need internet?

No. The app works completely offline since all processing happens on your device.

Does it need internet?

Yes. FireWatch AU needs an internet connection for current warnings and data. Offline, it shows the last information it downloaded, marked with how old it is.

Where does the information come from?

From every state and territory's fire and emergency agencies, the Bureau of Meteorology and other public sources. Each is credited in the app under Help & About, and each layer's page names its publishers.

Found a bug?

Please email support@benrichardson.dev with a description of what happened. If you can include your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce it, that helps a lot.

Have a feature idea?

I'd love to hear it. Send it to support@benrichardson.dev. I can't promise to build everything, but I do read all suggestions.

How do I delete my data?

Just uninstall the app. Since everything is stored locally on your device, deleting the app removes all data.

Which devices work?

The app runs on iPhone and iPad. Check the App Store listing for specific requirements.

Quick Troubleshooting

Before reaching out, try these:

  • Update to the latest version from the App Store
  • Check your internet connection: without one, FireWatch AU shows its last downloaded information
  • Restart the app
  • Make sure your device has enough storage
