Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this application handles your information. It covers all of Ben Richardson's apps, so some sections only apply to apps with that feature. The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and your data stays on your device.

Privacy at a Glance: This app does not collect, sell or share your personal data. What you create in it is stored on your device. Where a feature needs the internet, it sends only what that feature needs, and nothing is kept about you.

Information Collection

This app does not collect personal information. It works without:

  • User accounts or registration
  • Personal identification information
  • Contact information
  • Usage analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • Advertising or advertising identifiers

Data Storage

Data created within the app is stored locally on your device. This includes:

  • Any content you create or save, including history and results
  • App preferences and settings
  • Cache and temporary files

Where an app offers iCloud sync, that data is stored in your own private iCloud account, managed by Apple. We cannot see it.

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data through iOS Settings.

On-Device Processing

Some apps use Apple Intelligence or other on-device Apple frameworks (such as machine learning and network analysis) to provide their features. Where they do:

  • Processing happens on your iPhone or iPad
  • Your data is not sent to external AI services
  • Processing uses Apple's privacy-focused frameworks

Network Features and Our Servers

Some apps have features that need to connect to the internet, for example to test the network you're on or to look something up. Where they do:

  • The app sends only what the feature needs, and never your name, contacts, location or any identifier for you or your device.
  • Like any connection on the internet, the server that answers sees your device's public IP address while it responds.

Some of these features use small servers we run on Cloudflare Workers. For example, a server may tell an app which public IP address, internet provider and approximate location your connection appears to come from, so the app can show you where your traffic leaves the network. Our servers:

  • Answer the request and keep nothing: no logs, IP addresses or identifiers are stored
  • Send the answer only to your device
  • Are hosted by Cloudflare, which processes the traffic under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks or data brokers. There is:

  • No selling or sharing of your data
  • No advertisements
  • No tracking across apps or websites

Some features contact public services directly to do their job, such as well-known websites, public DNS resolvers or IP lookup services. These services see your IP address and the request, and handle it under their own privacy policies. The app does not send them anything that identifies you.

Where an app lets you choose an encrypted DNS provider (such as Cloudflare, Quad9, AdGuard, Mullvad or NextDNS), your device sends its DNS lookups directly to the provider you choose, under that provider's privacy policy. We never see your lookups.

Apple's standard frameworks and services are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Apps only ask for permissions a feature needs, and you can change them at any time in iOS Settings:

  • Location: iOS requires location permission to read the name of the Wi-Fi network you're connected to. Where an app asks for it for that reason, your location is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Local Network: used to find devices and services on your network, for example to check it for security problems. Nothing found is sent off your device.
  • Notifications: created on your device, for example to tell you the result of a check.
  • Calendars, Photos and other personal data: where an app asks for access, it uses that data on your device to provide the feature. We do not collect it.

In-App Purchases

Where an app offers in-app purchases, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details or Apple Account information; Apple only tells the app whether a purchase is active.

Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information, it is safe for users of all ages and complies with COPPA by design.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: Your data is stored on your device (or your iCloud account) and accessible only to you
  • Deletion: Remove all data by uninstalling the app
  • Portability: Your data exists only on your devices, under your control

For users in the EU, UK, Australia, California or other regions with privacy regulations: because we keep no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions, contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be noted in App Store update notes.
